## glass-voice-light

Image: glass-voice-light.png

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate one high-fidelity mobile app first-launch welcome/sign-in screen concept for the real app Clarity, a speaking practice app that follows spoken words and provides feedback. Deliver a single straight-on full-bleed portrait screen at approximately 1206x2622 aspect ratio. No physical phone frame, no surrounding presentation board, no captions outside UI. Show restrained iOS status bar at top and home indicator at bottom, accurate safe areas. Refined native Apple design with SF Pro Rounded typography, 34pt bold headline, 17pt body at a 402pt logical screen width. Maintain generous whitespace, 16-24pt margins, consistent capsule buttons and continuous rounded glass corners 28/36pt. Palette from existing app only: light background #F4F4F6, ink #111114, muted #77777E, accent #3478F6, pale blue #C4D5F6, white translucent glass; dark mode background #0B0B0D, white ink, muted #9E9EA6, accent #4C8DFF, wash #1E2E4B. No drop shadows on UI cards, depth through translucent glass. No decorative emoji or icon-library mashups. At bottom retain two full-width capsule authentication buttons with exact labels "Continue with Apple" and "Continue with Google" with their respective small brand symbols. These are the only main actions. No fabricated reviews, claims, ratings, or paywall. Copy perfectly legible. Brand wordmark "Clarity". This is a design exploration, not a screenshot of an existing implementation. Direction 1: Quiet and sculptural, light mode. Near-white canvas with subtle pale blue wash descending from top. Top wordmark centered. Hero in upper-middle: elegant abstract voice sculpture made of five upright translucent blue glass capsules of differing heights, like a soft dimensional waveform, floating without a platform. Refraction and pale blue light, no metallic chrome, no neon. Hero has generous negative space. Centered title beneath sculpture, exact text on two lines "Speak clearly." then "Sound like you." Supporting copy exact "Practice out loud. Get feedback." then "Find your rhythm." Bottom Apple button near-black with white text, Google button translucent white with dark text and a fine subtle border. No extra cards, no micro badges. Calm confident editorial composition. Main illustration upper 50 percent, copy next 20 percent, controls in bottom 20 percent.
